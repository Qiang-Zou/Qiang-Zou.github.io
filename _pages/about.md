I study the computational principles behind design and manufacturing, and build algorithms that put them into practice. My work spans geometric and physical modeling, intelligent design, and digital manufacturing.

The goal is to move from computer-*aided* to computer-**automated** design and manufacturing: **design by the computer, manufacturing in the computer**. I received my Ph.D. from the University of British Columbia.
